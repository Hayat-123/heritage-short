# ETHICS.md — The Coffee That Carries a Story

## Real People

No real person's face, voice, or likeness appears in this film in a way that requires consent, **with the following exceptions:**

| Person | What is used | Consent obtained? | How recorded |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Narrator (self or family member) | Pre-recorded Amharic voice narration | Yes — the narrator recorded these clips voluntarily for this project | In-person recording session |

**AI-generated characters:** The elderly woman and child visible in AI-generated keyframes are entirely fictional composites produced by FLUX.2 klein. They do not represent, resemble, or approximate any real person. No reference images of real individuals were provided to the model.
